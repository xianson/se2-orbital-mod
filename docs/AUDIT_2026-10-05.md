# Scenario audit, 2026-10-05

Five read-only auditors: player lifecycle, frame events, map and HUD, planning, planet transitions. Every finding was traced
in the code. Status: **fixed** (with an offline test, or with an in-game check), **open**, or **won't fix** (with the reason).
Tests: `Tests/PlayerOrbitTests` (offline, in the fast gate); in-game `tools/harness/hud_cases.sh`, `rv_cases.sh`,
`rv_views.sh`, `map_dim.sh`.

> **Status note, 2026-10-15** (checked against the code; the entries below are kept as written, with
> `[superseded ...]` marks where a later change replaced them). Since this log: the frame's size is 10 km
> (`ServerFrames.SlotRadius`; frames split past it, merges at 7.5 km, `RendezvousParams.EnterRangeMeters`), not 20 km; a
> frame is always anchored on a static grid or a rock in it (2026-10-07), so the 10-06 rock "reasons" and the dirty-slot
> move are gone; the berth stall is softened by `WarmBerths`. Still open: multiplayer, the second-world reload untested,
> and the FramesLock across the map draw and single-grid moves (2026-10-09 below). The current design is in
> `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Structural (the causes)
- [fixed] Player state was separate statics, each written in several branches. Now one `PlayerState`, built fresh every tick from its defaults and published whole (`Core/Frames/PlayerState.cs`, `FrameHost._cur/_pub`).
- [fixed] Client code read server entities. The server now publishes a grid snapshot every 5 ticks (`ServerFrames.View`: position, box, static flag, name, mass); the client reads the anchor box, static check, names and positions from it, and the cached aero beta instead of measuring on the server entity. `Contacts` followed on 2026-10-06 (below: `ServerFrames.View` / `SensorViews`).

## Player lifecycle
- [fixed] L1: a merge or shift requested while seated was replayed on standing up (thrown 100+ km). Requests are dropped while seated, on a new character and on a new session (`DropPending`).
- [fixed, offline test] L2: membership went stale when the seated ship split off or arrived. Seated, you leave any frame that isn't the seat's (`PlayerOrbit.KeepMembershipSeated`).
- [fixed, offline test] L3: game fast travel or a teleport out of a frame led to a split onto a bogus orbit. More than 60 km from the berth, you leave quietly (`PlayerOrbit.MovedOut`).
- [fixed] L4: joining a frame swallowed a queued stow. No joining while a stow is pending.
- [fixed, offline test] L5: the 300 m reach is measured from a grid's origin, not its hull (needs grid boxes in the server snapshot).
- [fixed] L6: a dead character's requests were replayed on the respawned one. Cleared on a new character.
- [fixed] L7: the player id wasn't kept current while seated, so a seated save lost membership.
- [fixed, in game] Standing up in a ship stowed with you seated left you in no frame (`PlayerOrbit.FrameToJoin`).

## 2026-10-07: six independent audits of the frame code (~58 findings), and why they were missed
Why: the suites tested one-grid ships in a clean creative world (the player flies a multi-grid scenario ship in a managed
world area); they checked states at chosen moments, not agreement over time (prediction vs motion); the same facts (membership,
anchor, joined ship, the x N warp scale, grid ids) live in many structures changed by a dozen paths; engine assumptions went
untested (stable ids, only the anchor pinned, nothing else acting on a steered ship); the glue was never tested offline; and
the author tested his own fixes. A harness bug (seatid seating from 6,000 km) also made every "get out" test meaningless.
Fixed:
- Identity: ships released from the game's managed world area before any move (it unloaded and respawned them under new ids);
  a server grid back in the scene within 60 s, same name, within 100 m, keeps its old id (tombstones); prunes and anchor
  handovers wait for it.
- Joined ships: the member loop no longer judges grids a split took this tick; a split takes the ship's unframed grids too;
  the heaviest grid of a ship anchors it; the whole ship's mass-weighted velocity is drained and every grid zeroed; stale joined
  sets dropped; a seat on a grid joined to the anchor is in the anchor.
- Anchors: no handover to the player while the anchor may be respawning (and position-only rebase - the velocity went in
  twice); rocks by their box centre; station keeping judged against the true pull at any warp (N^2 let every ship go in warp).
- Membership: the arrival carries only grids still its own; loose grids re-framed with encounters on too; a restored frame's
  moved berth takes its grids and the player at once.
- Warp: a ship with joints is steered as one body (it froze); thrust and bumps in warp reach the orbit; the seated plot uses the
  server's true relative velocity; the rider limit reads the jetpack's dampeners first.
- Threads and reloads: the server session per load; saved state per world; warp billing claimed atomically; snapshot reads
  under the lock; IsClear from the snapshot; no-throw AABBs.
New:
- Invariants (Frames/Invariants.cs): anchor-alive, past-edge, ship-together, warp-state, on-rails, seated-frame, prediction -
  checked once a second in play too, logged as [ORBIT-INV] VIOLATION; 'inv' in the harness.
- MembershipRules (Core/Frames, game-free) with a fake world (Tests/PlayerOrbitTests/MembershipCases.cs; 18 cases then, grouped in 10 numbered groups now): anchor
  election, split hand-off, still-judged, arrival carry, prune - and ServerFrames runs these rules (GridWorld).
- tools/harness/hercules.sh (the player's own multi-grid ship) and tools/harness/fuzz.sh (seeded random play against the
  invariants).

- In-game scenario script `tools/harness/scenarios.sh`:
  - S1 anchor destroyed: new anchor, 0.2 m from its orbit (no jump);
  - S2 a ship moved 150 km out leaves quietly;
  - S3 seated through a split: you leave the old frame, and on standing up you are in the ship's frame with no split of your own;
  - S4 a pasted grid is adopted with encounters off;
  - S5 warp into a rendezvous.
- Design notes:
  - merges are decided by frame centres, but riding far from your anchor you split off at 20 km first (10 km now), so you still merge once near;
  - the Lagrange preview treats a frozen burn as one impulse (small error);
  - a ship with dampeners on station-keeps in its frame (by design), so it does not follow its own orbit there.

## 2026-10-09: the three open items (code and offline tests only; no game run)
- [fixed, offline test; in-game check pending] OWN-ORBIT SMOOTHING. Your own orbit while riding (the plan's base, Maneuvers.Base,
  and the planet-space local orbit, FrameHost.TryGetLocalOrbit) was captured from ONE noisy measurement and kept until a
  measurement left it by 25 m / 0.05 m/s - the plan trailed the ship by up to ~860 m a revolution, and a tighter gate re-solved
  every tick. `RendezvousPlot.OwnOrbit` now fits the last ~4 s of measurements (residuals from the kept orbit, a weighted
  least-squares state at now: linear over the window, the gravity gradient far under the noise) and re-solves when the fit
  leaves the kept orbit by more than 4 standard errors (scaled by the residuals' own scatter; floors 2 m / 0.002 m/s, never
  wider than the old gate). A real change - a measurement further from the fit than the old 25 m / 0.05 m/s (a burn, a bump)
  - drops the window and starts from that measurement at once; a gap longer than the window (warp) is the old gate; several
  callers in one tick count as one measurement. The HUD's drawn orbit and drift plot (OrbitDisplay, a fresh capture every
  frame before) use the same fit (`Maneuvers.OwnOrbitDrawn`, its own instance). Offline (Tests/PlayerOrbitTests, RendezvousCases
  `OwnOrbitSmoothing`), noise +-1 m / +-0.02 m/s per axis, 60 s, 6 seeds: a revolution on the kept orbit is 12 m off the ship
  (the single-measurement gate: 307 m), 4 solves in 60 s (old: 49); a steady 0.1 m/s^2 burn trails by 0.055 m/s (old 0.052);
  warp solves as before; a 1 m/s burn solves at once. To check in game: the HUD plot and the plan steady while coasting, and
  no lag a revolution on (DevHudCheck "the plan starts from you" keeps its 30 m / 0.1 m/s); a burn's readout still converges.
- [partly fixed] FramesLock HELD ACROSS SLOW GAME CALLS. Done (collected under the lock, acted on after it, the same tick):
  - asteroid rock volumes (AsteroidFrames): the locked half plans (ring rocks made sites, which rocks are wanted - the
    frames' reads); `AsteroidFrames.ServerAfterLock`, called from ServerFrames.Tick right after the frame step's lock, does the
    generator's work - stale volumes reconciled, the sector rebuild (Deactivate + CreateTriggers), SpawnEntity / DeleteEntity,
    the rock count over the generator's bodies. A ring rock left behind still frees its site and slot in the same pass, under

- [fixed, offline test; in-game check pending] ROCK-ANCHORED FRAMES ON UNSTABLE ORBITS. Rule (MembershipRules.StaticAnchorKind,
  OrbitStable - game-free): a static grid anchors a frame on any orbit (on an unstable one it is made a ship first, as before);
  a rock anchors one only on a stable orbit (periapsis 5 km over the planet's border, bound). A rock cannot be made a ship nor
  moved: a frame decaying into an atmosphere anchored on one was "the rock's orbit" going down while the rock stayed in its
  berth. Unstable, the rock is left out of the frame (just scenery): its heaviest player grid anchors it, else the player -
  the path a deleted rock already took (ElectAnchor from AsteroidAnchorId, the rebase without a jump). No hysteresis needed: a
  rock-anchored frame's orbit is fixed (nothing folds into a static anchor's frame). Offline: MembershipCases case 10 (stable,
  inside the border, inside the margin, escape, NaN; rock / grid / none; the election after the rock is let go). To check in
  game: a ship descending past a rock near its berth is not captured by it (log "not anchored on its rock").
